Licence: https://github.com/Shawn-Shan/fawkes/blob/master/LICENSE . Its README describes personal privacy protection and academic research as intended uses. Upstream model weights have their own provenance; review before deployment.

FaceNet-PyTorch is by Tim Esler and contributors: https://github.com/timesler/facenet-pytorch . Its code is MIT licensed. The evaluator uses InceptionResnetV1/vggface2 weights; review their model/data terms separately.

The optional real-cloak smoke test uses scikit-image's NASA astronaut example. It tests changed output pixels, not identification or training protection.
